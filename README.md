This paper investigates the predefined-time formation control for nonlinear multi-agent systems with model uncertainties. A tunable predefined-time distributed behavioral control scheme is developed by embedding a tunable predefined-time criterion into the null-space-based behavioral projection mechanism. Unlike existing distributed behavioral control methods that rely on unknown or fixed convergence bounds, the proposed approach enables explicit and flexible online tuning of the settling time through extra tunable parameters. Each agent coordinates two prioritized distributed behaviors to guarantee task-space convergence within a user-specified predefined time, while allowing the convergence speed to be regulated online without affecting system stability. Furthermore, a tunable predefined-time sliding-mode controller is designed to ensure accurate behavioral trajectory tracking in the presence of uncertainties. The proposed method achieves decentralized formation control with tunable predefined-time convergence that is independent of the initial conditions. Simulation results demonstrate that the closed-loop system satisfies tunable predefined-time bounds, thereby validating the effectiveness and superiority of the proposed scheme.
